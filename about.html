About

A local's honest take on Sabah

My name is Fong. I'm a Sabahan who was born, grew up, and still lives in Kota Kinabalu. I work from home, which means I spend a lot of time actually in this city: eating at the same places, trying new ones, watching what opens and what closes.

This blog started because every time someone from outside Sabah asked me "where should I go?" I found myself writing the same long WhatsApp message. Better to just put it here.

I'm into food, badminton, pickleball, and fitness. A lot of the content here reflects that. I'll also write about whatever else feels worth documenting: living costs, day trips, the honest version of "is KK good for digital nomads" (yes, with caveats).

No sponsored content. No paid placements. If I say a place is good, it's because I actually think it's good.

I'm also an amateur photographer, so the photos won't always be perfect. But they're all taken by me, on the ground, at the actual places.

If you have a question, a tip, or just want to say hi, feel free to message me about anything. Always happy to hear from people.
