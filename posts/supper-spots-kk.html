The Best Supper Spots in KK: Where Locals Actually Go After 10pm

Best Food 6 min read

KK comes alive late. The city has a proper supper culture and if you're only eating dinner by 7pm you're missing half of it.

KK's night food scene starts properly after 10pm

KK doesn't sleep early. By 10pm the supper spots are filling up, and by midnight the serious ones are packed. This is when the city feels most itself: loud, unhurried, eating things that are too good to be this cheap.

If you're only eating at restaurants that take reservations, you're missing the best food in the city.

Sinsuran Night Market Area

Sinsuran at midnight. Follow the queues.

The most reliable late-night eating ground in KK. Stalls run until 2 or 3am. The mix is good: Malay, Chinese, local Sabahan dishes, grilled things on sticks, bowls of soup that have been simmering since afternoon.

Look for the stall with the longest queue. In KK nobody queues for bad food.

Lintas Square

Where the KK middle class goes for supper. Proper tables and chairs, hawker stalls that have been running long enough to know what they're doing. The seafood is consistently good. Butter prawns, steamed fish, fried tofu, done well.

Butter prawns at Lintas. This is the order.

Goes crowded on weekends. Go weekdays if you want a seat without waiting.

Sang Nyuk Mee Near Centre Point

An old kopitiam near Centre Point that's been running late nights for as long as anyone can remember. The specialty is Sang Nyuk Mee, pork noodle soup. The broth is the thing. They start it in the morning and by midnight it's at its most concentrated.

Ask any KK local to point you to the sang nyuk mee place near Centre Point and they'll know exactly which one.

Foh Sang Area, Luyang

Char siu from Foh Sang. The standard everything else in KK is measured against.

The local's local. Further from the tourist belt, no frills, some of the best late-night Chinese food in KK. The char siu here is the standard against which most other char siu in the city is measured. Worth the Grab ride.

What to Order Everywhere

Hinava if it's available. Raw fish cured in lime juice with ginger and bird's eye chilli. One of those things that tastes completely of Sabah and nowhere else.

Tuaran Mee. A local noodle dish, fried with egg and simple seasoning. The best ones have a smoky wok hei you can smell from three tables away.

The Honest Take

The best supper in KK costs between RM15 and RM40 per person. You won't eat better for that price anywhere in Malaysia. That's not loyalty talking. That's just accurate. Go late. Eat a lot.
